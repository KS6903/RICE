# Template: Employee privacy notice

**For the mill to issue, not for {{provider_name}} to issue.**

You, the mill, are the Personal Information Controller for your workers' records. Sections 6.2
and 9.3 of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) make giving this notice your responsibility.
Issuing it, and keeping a dated record that you did, is the single cheapest protection you have
against a data privacy complaint from a worker.

Copy the text below onto your letterhead, fill in every `[ ]`, translate it into the language your
workers actually read (a Tagalog, Cebuano or Ilocano version is usually the right call), hand a copy
to each worker, and keep a dated record that you did in their 201 file.

This template is a starting point, not legal advice. Have your own counsel review it.

---

## [ MILL NAME ] — Privacy Notice for Employees and Workers

**Effective [ date ]**

### Who is responsible for your information

[ Mill name ], with office at [ address ], is responsible for the personal information described
here. We are the Personal Information Controller under Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act
of 2012.

Our Data Protection Officer / privacy contact is [ name ], [ email ], [ phone ].

### What we collect about you

- Your name, address, telephone number, email address and photograph.
- Your birthdate, civil status and emergency contact.
- Your government identifier numbers: SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and TIN.
- Your position, branch, hire date, employment status, salary, attendance and payroll records.
- Records of the work you do that the mill records, such as batches you operate, deliveries you
  drive, and entries you make in our systems.

Your government identifier numbers and your birthdate are **sensitive personal information** under
the Data Privacy Act. We handle them with extra care and restrict who can see them.

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|

| To remit SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and withholding tax, and to file the returns the law requires | Compliance with a legal obligation, section 12(c), and for sensitive personal information, section 13(b), which permits processing required by laws on employment, social security, health insurance and taxation |
| To keep records the Department of Labor and Employment and the Bureau of Internal Revenue require us to keep | Legal obligation |
| To keep the mill, its stock and its people safe, and to investigate incidents | Our legitimate interest, section 12(f) |
| [ any other purpose you actually have ] | [ basis ] |

We do not use your information for any purpose you have not been told about here.

### Who we share it with

- **Government agencies**: SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR and DOLE, as the law requires.
- **Our service providers**, who process on our instructions under contract, including
  {{provider_legal_name}}, which operates the {{product_name}} mill management system where your

- **[ your bank or payroll provider, if any ]**, to pay your salary.
- **Courts, regulators or law enforcement**, where a valid order or legal obligation requires it.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for anyone's marketing.

### Where it is kept

Your records are kept in the {{product_name}} system, which stores data in [ region, ask

### How long we keep it

We keep your records for the duration of your employment and for [ number ] years afterwards, which
is the period our tax, labour and social security obligations require. Payroll and tax records are
kept for 10 years. After that we delete or anonymise them.

### Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to object, to access your
information, to have it corrected, to have it erased or blocked in the circumstances the law
allows, to damages for unlawful processing, and to obtain a copy in a portable electronic format.

To exercise any of these, contact [ name ], [ email ], [ phone ]. We will acknowledge within 5
working days and respond within 15 calendar days.

You may also complain to the **National Privacy Commission**, 5th Floor Delegation Building, PICC
Complex, Roxas Boulevard, Pasay City, complaints@privacy.gov.ph.

### If there is a data breach

If a breach happens that puts your information at risk, we will notify the National Privacy
Commission and you within 72 hours of knowing about it, as the law requires.
